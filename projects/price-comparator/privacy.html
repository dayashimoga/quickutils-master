Welcome to Price Comparator. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifying information. Search queries are processed in your browser to fetch prices, but we do not store your search history on our servers. We use third-party tools like Google Analytics which collect anonymized usage data.

2. Cookies

We use cookies through our third-party partners (like Google AdSense) to serve personalized advertisements based on your prior visits to our website.

3. Third-Party Links

Our tool links to external e-commerce sites (Amazon, Flipkart, etc). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

4. Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page.
