## ChessOS Unique Advantages
1. **Zero-cost, zero-backend** — runs entirely client-side
2. **Knowledge Graph** — prerequisite-based concept learning (unique among all platforms)
3. **Boss Battles with certification** — gamified mastery verification
4. **Integrated visualization training** — 4 trainers in one platform
5. **SM-2 spaced repetition** for openings (Chessable-like but free)

## Known Gaps vs Industry Leaders
1. **Puzzle database size** — 20 puzzles vs 100K+ (Chess.com/Lichess)
2. **Multiplayer** — AI-only tournaments vs real opponents
3. **Opening database** — 50+ openings vs full ECO database (3000+)
4. **Engine analysis depth** — CDN Stockfish vs server-side deep analysis
5. **Mobile app** — Web-only vs native iOS/Android apps
6. **Social features** — Local-only vs real multiplayer social graph
