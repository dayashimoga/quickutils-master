📚 Chemistry Fundamentals

🔬 Atoms & Elements

Atoms are the building blocks of matter. Elements are pure substances made of only one type of atom, organized in the periodic table.

⚗️ Chemical Reactions

Reactions occur when substances interact to form new substances. Energy is either absorbed (endothermic) or released (exothermic).

⚖️ Conservation of Mass

Matter is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction — the mass of reactants equals the mass of products.

🌡️ pH & Acids/Bases

pH measures acidity on a 0-14 scale. Below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral, and above 7 is basic (alkaline).
